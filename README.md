Dustline is an original 2D top-down open-world crime game set in San Ceniza, a bleached high-noon desert border town: one long highway strip of truck stops, motels and a chapel, a dry arroyo that serves as a shortcut network, mesa country to the west, and a border checkpoint to the south. You are June Calloway, an ex-border-patrol mechanic turned smuggler, working with Wrench (engine boost) and Lookout (a scanner that reveals patrol vision cones). Seven story runs — contraband deliveries, a decoy run, a water-tower cover job and a dawn finale in a loaded semi — pay out through five district buyers, and cargo is worth more the longer you dare to hold it.

The law answers in kind: heat rises from a single patrol car through PIT manoeuvres, checkpoint roadblocks, the DUSTOFF-1 helicopter with its searchlight and spike strips, up to tactical trucks. On foot June aims with the mouse and carries a revolver, shotgun, rifle and SMG; on the road she can steal sedans, pickups and heavy semi trucks, race rivals down the strip, and spend cash at the garage on armour, engine and off-road tyres. Heat decays off-road in the arroyo, pedestrians scatter, traffic fills the highway, and a synth radio ticker, minimap and full HUD keep every system visible at once. Progress auto-saves to localStorage, and all sound — engine, sirens, shots and wind — is synthesised with WebAudio. No network requests, no external assets: everything is drawn and sounded in code.

Controls: WASD move/drive, mouse aim, click to shoot, E enter/exit vehicles, Shift for Wrench boost, Q switch weapon, R start a street race near the strip, G garage, M atlas, P/Esc pause.

## Run it

```
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.
